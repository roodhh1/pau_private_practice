About

I am a physician and psychiatrist with a high-specialty in neuropsychiatry, trained at Mexico's National Institute of Neurology and Neurosurgery (INNN) and UNAM. For the past several years I have run a private telehealth practice, caring for adults with mood and anxiety disorders, ADHD, and stress-related conditions — combining a rigorous clinical foundation with a warm, counseling-oriented approach.

My background sits at the intersection of brain, behavior, and emotional well-being. I completed additional training at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid in evidence-based treatment of the psychopathology of emotions, stress, and health, and I have published peer-reviewed research on neuropsychiatric symptoms in Alzheimer's disease.

Now based in the San Francisco Bay Area as a U.S. permanent resident, I am expanding into the U.S. mental health space in non-prescribing roles — telehealth, clinical program design, medical advising, research, and initiatives serving Spanish-speaking communities. I bring years of remote, cross-cultural care and fluency in Spanish, English, and French.

Open to connecting with leaders in mental health, digital health, and telemedicine. 📩
