About

A medical specialist's depth, offered as guidance.

I'm Dr. Paulina Lebrija, a physician and psychiatrist with a high specialty in neuropsychiatry, trained at Mexico's National Institute of Neurology and Neurosurgery and UNAM, with over a decade of clinical experience.

Now based in the San Francisco Bay Area, I work with people across the U.S. in a non-prescribing capacity — helping you understand what you're experiencing, make sense of a diagnosis, manage stress and emotions, and navigate your options with confidence.

Think of me as an expert guide and educator who listens. For medication or formal treatment you'll always work with a licensed local provider; my role is to help you feel informed, steady, and supported — in Spanish or English.
